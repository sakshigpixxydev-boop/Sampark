# School User Guide - Pramukh Sankalp School Management System

## Introduction

Welcome to the Pramukh Sankalp School Management System. As a School user, you can manage your school's students, view payments, and track invoices.

## Getting Started

### Logging In

1. Navigate to the login page
2. Enter your username
3. Choose authentication method:
   - **Password**: Enter your password
   - **PIN**: Enter your 4-digit PIN
4. Click "Login"

### Dashboard Overview

After logging in, you'll see the School Dashboard with:

- **Overview Card**: Total students enrolled in your school
- **Student Payments**: Pending payments for your students
- **New Inquiries**: Recent student registrations with payment status filters

**Important**: You can only view and manage students belonging to your school.

## Managing Students

### Viewing Your Students

1. Navigate to **Students**
2. View list of all students enrolled in your school
3. Use filters to find specific students:
   - Payment Status
   - Standard
   - Student Type
   - City

### Student Information

For each student, you can view:
- Personal information (name, email, contact)
- Guardian information
- Enrollment details (standard, batch, type)
- Payment status
- Invoice history
- Parcel tracking

### Adding Students

1. Navigate to **Students** → **Create Student**
2. Fill in required fields:
   - Student Type
   - Educational Board
   - Standard
   - Taluka
   - First Name, Middle Name, Last Name
   - Email (must be unique)
3. Optional fields:
   - Gender, Birthdate
   - Address, Contact Numbers
4. Click **Save**

### Editing Student Information

1. Navigate to **Students**
2. Click **Edit** on the student row
3. Update information
4. Click **Save**

## Guardian Management

### Viewing Guardians

1. Navigate to **Guardians**
2. View list of all guardians for your students
3. Click on a guardian to see contact information and associated students

### Adding Guardians

1. Navigate to **Guardians** → **Create Guardian**
2. Fill in required fields:
   - Relation (Father, Mother, Guardian, etc.)
   - First Name, Middle Name, Last Name
3. Add contact information
4. Associate with student
5. Click **Save**

**Note**: Multiple guardians can be added per student.

### Updating Guardian Information

1. Navigate to **Guardians**
2. Click **Edit** on the guardian row
3. Update information
4. Click **Save**

## Payment Management

### Viewing Pending Payments

1. Navigate to **Dashboard** → **Student Payments** section
2. View students with pending payments
3. Click on a student to view payment details

### Payment Status

- **Pending**: Payment not yet received
- **Authorized**: Payment approved but not completed
- **Paid**: Payment successfully completed
- **Failed**: Payment transaction failed

### Coordinating Payments

1. Identify students with pending payments
2. Contact students/guardians for payment
3. Coordinate with system administrator or accountant for payment processing
4. Verify payment status after processing

**Note**: As a School user, you can view payment status but cannot process payments directly. Contact an Accountant or Admin for payment processing.

## Invoice Management

### Viewing Invoices

1. Navigate to **Invoices**
2. View all invoices for your students
3. Filter by:
   - Student
   - Status
   - Date range

### Invoice Details

Each invoice shows:
- Student information
- Fee breakdown
- Discounts applied
- Total amount due
- Payment status

### Invoice Status

- **DRAFT**: Not yet issued
- **ISSUED**: Sent to student/school
- **PARTIAL**: Partially paid
- **PAID**: Fully paid
- **VOID**: Cancelled

### Downloading Invoices

1. Navigate to **Invoices**
2. Click on an invoice
3. Click **Download** or **Print**

## Parcel Tracking

### Viewing Parcels

1. Navigate to **Parcels**
2. View all parcels sent to your students
3. Click tracking link to view shipment status

### Parcel Information

For each parcel, you can view:
- Student name
- Parcel company
- Tracking number
- Tracking link
- Notes

## Profile Management

### Updating Your Profile

1. Click on your name in the top right
2. Select **Profile**
3. Update your information
4. Click **Save**

### Changing Password

1. Go to **Profile** → **Change Password**
2. Enter current password
3. Enter new password
4. Confirm new password
5. Click **Update**

### Changing PIN

1. Go to **Profile** → **Change PIN**
2. Enter current PIN
3. Enter new 4-digit PIN
4. Confirm new PIN
5. Click **Update**

### Forgot Password/PIN

1. On the login page, click **Forgot Password** or **Forgot PIN**
2. Enter your email address
3. Check your email for reset instructions
4. Follow the link to reset your password/PIN

## Search and Filtering

### Searching Students

Use the search bar to quickly find students by:
- Name
- Email

### Filtering Students

Use the filter panel to narrow down results by:
- Payment Status
- Standard
- Student Type
- City

### Clearing Filters

Click **Clear All Filters** to reset and view all students.

## Best Practices

1. **Keep Information Current**: Regularly update student and guardian contact information
2. **Monitor Payments**: Check pending payments regularly
3. **Verify Invoices**: Review invoices for accuracy
4. **Track Parcels**: Monitor parcel deliveries to students
5. **Communication**: Maintain open communication with students and guardians

## Common Tasks

### Enrolling a New Student

1. Collect student information
2. Navigate to **Students** → **Create Student**
3. Fill in all required fields
4. Add guardian information
5. Submit and verify student appears in your list

### Following Up on Pending Payments

1. Navigate to **Dashboard**
2. Check **Student Payments** section
3. Identify students with pending payments
4. Contact students/guardians
5. Coordinate payment with accountant

### Checking Invoice Status

1. Navigate to **Invoices**
2. Find the student's invoice
3. Check status and payment details
4. Download invoice if needed

### Tracking a Parcel

1. Navigate to **Parcels**
2. Find the student's parcel
3. Click the tracking link
4. View shipment status on carrier website

## Troubleshooting

### Cannot See a Student
- Verify the student is enrolled in your school
- Contact administrator if student should be in your school

### Payment Not Showing
- Check if payment was recently processed (may take time to update)
- Contact accountant to verify payment status
- Check invoice status

### Cannot Edit Student
- Verify you have permission to edit
- Check if student is locked for processing
- Contact administrator for assistance

### Invoice Not Generated
- Verify student has required information
- Check if fee plan exists for the student
- Contact administrator to generate invoice

## Limitations

As a School user, you:
- Can only view and manage students in your school
- Cannot process payments directly (Accountant role required)
- Cannot manage other schools or system configuration (Admin role required)
- Cannot approve upload batches (Accountant role required)
- Cannot manage users (Admin role required)
